Making legislative and policy effects observable, comparable, and explainable.

My research brings legislative studies and constitutional and administrative law into dialogue with large language models and social simulation. I develop general-purpose, reproducible platforms for evaluating complex legislative and policy scenarios, comparing alternatives, and improving policy design.
