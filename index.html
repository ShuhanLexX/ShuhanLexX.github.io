<p class="scholar-hero__status">Interdisciplinary research on legislation, generative social simulation, and evidence-informed policy design.</p>

I study how legislative and policy questions can be examined as complex social systems before rules are put into practice.

My work connects constitutional and administrative law with generative AI, social simulation, and empirical validation. I develop reproducible research workflows for comparing policy scenarios, tracing trade-offs, and supporting more transparent legislative and policy evaluation.

Research Highlights

A guiding question: how can we make the anticipated effects of legislation and public policy more observable, comparable, and explainable?

    <h3>Generative-agent social simulation</h3>
    <p>I build theory-informed LLM agents that represent heterogeneous cognition, interaction, and policy response, allowing social dynamics to be explored in transparent and reproducible simulation settings.</p>
  </div>
</article>
<article class="research-theme">

  <div>
    <h3>Policy evaluation and empirical validation</h3>
    <p>I combine simulation experiments with real-world data and statistical methods to compare interventions, test robustness, and inform policy optimisation.</p>
