Legislative Studies Generative Social Simulation Policy Evaluation

I work at the intersection of legislative studies, generative AI, and computational social science. My research turns complex policy questions into transparent, reproducible social simulations—so that policy effects, trade-offs, and possible improvements can be examined before implementation.

Featured research: GPLab

As first and corresponding author, I co-developed GPLab, a generative agent-based framework for policy simulation and evaluation. It connects LLM-driven social agents, modular social subsystems, and an iterative simulation–evaluation loop.

Original Figure 1 from GPLab: A Generative Agent-Based Framework for Policy Simulation and Evaluation, JASSS 29(1), 2026. View the publication.

Research profile

Legislative studies

Connecting constitutional and administrative law with the institutional design and evaluation of legislation.

Generative social simulation

Building theory-informed LLM agents to model heterogeneous individuals, cognition, interaction, and policy response.

Policy evaluation

Combining simulation, real-world data, and statistical validation to compare interventions and support policy design.

Selected contributions

Generative-agent policy evaluation

GPLab provides reusable infrastructure for scenario configuration, cross-system experiments, and multi-dimensional evaluation. The published work is available through the JASSS article and its open-source repository.
