I work at the intersection of legislative studies, generative AI, and computational social science. My research turns complex policy questions into transparent, reproducible social simulations—so that policy effects, trade-offs, and possible improvements can be examined before implementation.

## Research Directions

<div class="feature-grid" markdown="0">
