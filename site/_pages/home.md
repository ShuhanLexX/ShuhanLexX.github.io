I work at the intersection of legislative studies, generative AI, and computational social science. My research turns complex policy questions into transparent, reproducible social simulations—so that policy effects, trade-offs, and possible improvements can be examined before implementation.

<div class="callout callout-info" markdown="0">
<div class="callout-title"><i class="fa-solid fa-flask callout-icon"></i> Featured research: GPLab</div>
<p>As first and corresponding author, I co-developed <strong>GPLab</strong>, a generative agent-based framework for policy simulation and evaluation. It connects LLM-driven social agents, modular social subsystems, and an iterative simulation–evaluation loop.</p>
</div>
