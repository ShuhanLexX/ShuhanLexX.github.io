<div class="home-introduction" markdown="0">
  <p class="home-introduction__lead">I study how legislative and policy questions can be examined as complex social systems before rules are put into practice.</p>
  <p>My work connects constitutional and administrative law with generative AI, social simulation, and empirical validation. I develop reproducible research workflows for comparing policy scenarios, tracing trade-offs, and supporting more transparent legislative and policy evaluation.</p>
</div>

<section class="homepage-section" aria-labelledby="research-highlights" markdown="0">
  <h2 id="research-highlights">Research Highlights</h2>
  <p class="research-question">A guiding question: how can we make the anticipated effects of legislation and public policy more observable, comparable, and explainable?</p>

  <div class="research-theme-list">
    <article class="research-theme">
      <span class="research-theme__number">01</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Legislation and institutional design</h3>
        <p>I examine the constitutional basis, institutional mechanisms, and implementation logic of legislation, with attention to how legal rules shape public coordination and social outcomes.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-theme">
      <span class="research-theme__number">02</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Generative-agent social simulation</h3>
        <p>I build theory-informed LLM agents that represent heterogeneous cognition, interaction, and policy response, allowing social dynamics to be explored in transparent and reproducible simulation settings.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-theme">
      <span class="research-theme__number">03</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Policy evaluation and empirical validation</h3>
        <p>I combine simulation experiments with real-world data and statistical methods to compare interventions, test robustness, and inform policy optimisation.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
