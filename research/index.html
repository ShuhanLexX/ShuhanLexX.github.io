Research Interests

My research develops a connected workflow for studying policy as a complex social process. Legal questions guide simulation design; empirical data help assess the results; reusable software makes the workflow accessible and reproducible.

01

Legislative studies

Constitutional foundations, legislative design, and the institutional mechanisms through which legal rules shape social outcomes.

02

LLM-based social simulation

Generative agents and interacting social subsystems for studying heterogeneous behavior and complex social dynamics.

03

Legislation & policy evaluation

Reproducible policy experiments, real-world data, and statistical validation to compare scenarios and improve proposed interventions.
