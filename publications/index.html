2026

  1. JASSS
    GPLab: A Generative Agent-Based Framework for Policy Simulation and Evaluation
    Shuhan Zhang, Zifan Peng, and Yinwang Ren
    Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML Code

    GPLab is a general-purpose framework that integrates generative large language models with agent-based modelling for policy simulation and evaluation. It combines boundedly rational social agents, modular social subsystems, and a simulation–evaluation loop to support cross-domain policy experiments.
